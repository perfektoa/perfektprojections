## 2. Organization Builder (new app page, `/organization`)
`tgs-viz/src/pages/OrganizationPage.jsx` + `src/lib/orgBuilder.js`. All teams, league-switchable, read-only on the JSON. Builds MLB **5 SP / 8 RP / 13** with your position distribution; minors **6 SP / 9 RP** + a hit-gated lineup (a hitter only rosters at a level where his *offense* holds up); playing time prioritized by potential; position log-jams cascade down a level ("split"); empty spots flagged for a filler.

**Pitcher roles are level-aware** (`pitcherRole`, fixed this session): at MLB a pitcher plays the role he projects best in *now* (a guy better in short relief relieves). In the **minors** any *starter-capable* arm (the sheet's `Starter` flag = enough pitches + stamina) is developed as **SP**, ranked into the rotation by potential; only relief-only arms (`Starter=FALSE`) fill the bullpen. Without this every minor-leaguer graded out better as RP on current value (prospects get unloaded on the 3rd time through), so the minor rotations were coming up empty — possible starters now actually start.

**Minor-league construction philosophy** (reworked this session — win + develop, with depth and leaders):
- **Full ladder, WL as the floor** (`LEVELS = ["INT","WL","R-",…,"MLB"]`). The org builder constructs a team card for every rung, including the two bottom ones: **WL** (winter league — the lowest *regular* rung, the floor for everyone) and **INT** (international complex — one below WL, reachable only by international-signed `Lev INT` players). Every player is placed at the level his ability fits; a too-raw player drops to WL (or INT for intl guys), never lands in depth above the floor. Enforced via `floorRank` (`minLev` = INT for `Lev INT`, else WL), which guards both initial placement AND the cascade so a player never spills below his floor.
- **Age caps per level** (`MAX_AGE_BY_LEVEL`): WL ≤19, R- ≤21, R+ ≤23, A- ≤25, A+ ≤27; INT ≤19; AA/AAA/MLB any. A player too old for a rung is pushed UP to the lowest level he's young enough for; if his ability can't hold that level he's org filler → depth. Enforced through `floorRank` (= max of holding-level floor and age floor), which gates both initial bucketing and the cascade, so no roster can contain an over-age player. Verified 0 violations org-wide with rosters still full.
- **Current-location chip (`LocChip`) beside every player** — the cards are *prescriptive* (where a player should be), so each line also shows where he **is right now** in OOTP, to find him in-game: muted **@ {lev}** when he's at that level (or elsewhere, e.g. a 40-man guy on the AAA card reads `@ MLB`), green **↑ {lev}** when his ability places him *above* his current level = promote him (e.g. `↑ A+` on the AAA card). The ↑ is the "when to promote" readout. The demote direction is deliberately *not* shown — it's dominated by 40-man depth (`Lev MLB` carries ~31 Cubs pitchers) placed at their true ability level, which isn't a meaningful demote; those just read as a neutral `@ MLB` location.
- **Prove-it-or-lose-it.** An orange **prove it** tag flags an INT-team player aged ≥ 18 — old enough that staying in the complex is promote-or-release territory (threshold tunable in `Tags`). Younger raw guys just sit and develop.
- UI: `Tags` in `OrganizationPage.jsx` (★/☆ captain, ↑ promote, prove-it); ↑/prove-it use `LEVEL_RANK` to compare a player's `Lev` to the card's level.
- **Play time = win + grow blend, emphasis winning.** Minor starters (and the SP/RP order) are chosen on `blendScore = 0.6·cur + 0.4·pot` (`WIN_W`/`GROW_W`, tunable). The best win-now-and-high-ceiling guys start; raw high-ceiling guys who get blocked cascade DOWN to keep starting (play time develops them) rather than benching.

Coverage is best-effort ("not always possible" — e.g. a level whose pool has no fitting captain stays uncovered rather than force-seating an overmatched player).

## Win-model lenses on the Roster Optimizer (this session)
A **model selector** (segmented buttons, top-right of the page) re-scores the SAME roster through five win models so the user can see *more ways* to read "most wins." - **Runs foundation.** Real RS/RA reconstructed from columns already in the JSON: offense = lineup `BatR`+`BSR` (PA-weighted by the vR share), run environment = each MLB team's **actual** staff (top-5 SP by `WAA wtd` + top-8 RP by `WAA wtd RP`, innings-weighted 62/38, averaged league-wide → ~**4.47 R/G**; this avoids the 40-man scrub tail that inflates a naive mean to 5.13). `RPW = 1.5·lgRG + 3` (Tango). **RS−RA is pinned to the linear run differential** `(weightedLineupWAA + pitcherWAA)·RPW`, so Pythagorean and Linear agree at average and differ only by the curve — never by a bookkeeping gap.
- **Linear** — the existing `81 + WAA` (unchanged default).
- **Pythagorean** — PythagenPat `x=(R/G)^0.287`, `Win%=RS^x/(RS^x+RA^x)`. Cubs: linear 92 → pyth **93** (+1, as expected; the two only diverge in the tails, e.g. ALL-orgs 127→122).
- **Monte Carlo** — `monteCarloSeason()`, 2,500 seeded (mulberry32 + Box-Muller, reproducible) seasons: each perturbs RS/RA by projection uncertainty, draws a Pythagorean true-talent win%, plays 162 Bernoulli games. Returns median/floor(p5)/ceiling(p95) + `pGE85..pGE100` odds + a win histogram. Cubs: median 93, floor 81, ceiling 105, P(90+) 68%. **NOTE: the "90-win playoff line" is a proxy, not a real league sim** (labeled as such in the UI). - **Durability** — `durabilityWeighted:true` flag haircuts each player's value columns by a playing-time factor (`PRONE_FACTOR`: Iron Man .99 … Wrecked .68, × current-DL penalty) via `applyDurability`, then **re-runs the same selection** (a durable solid bat can beat a fragile star) and re-scores.
